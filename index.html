01About

I help enterprises bring order and speed to complex, high-stakes IT transformations — the kind where a missed release gate, a poorly sequenced cutover, or unclear governance can cost millions and derail timelines.

Most recently I led Release and Change for SHERPA X, Siemens' group-wide SAP S/4HANA transformation program — owning the release governance model, cutover sequencing, and cross-tribe coordination, and authoring the program's master release numbering standard. Before that, I ran release management for Fiserv's payments platforms out of Frankfurt and Nuremberg, and held governance and platform operations roles at Wirecard and DBS Singapore.

What I bring beyond standard release/PMO delivery is a practical instinct for automation: I built ERSA (Enterprise Release Stability Agent), a LangChain-based agentic AI tool that supports release governance decisioning — bringing faster, data-backed risk assessments into a discipline that traditionally runs on spreadsheets and manual sign-offs.

I work well in regulated, high-governance environments, communicate directly about scope and risk rather than overpromising, and I'm comfortable stepping into ambiguous programs and building structure from the ground up.
